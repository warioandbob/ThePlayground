Prose Mood Cloud

Since I was 10, I was writing novels. One quote that's stuck with me is that the goal of fiction is "to give your reader a powerful emotional experience." I thought it'd be fun to explore that literally by presenting small passages from my novels through the emotion they invoke. Click an emotion in the cloud, get a random passage from one of my 11 novels over 20 years.
